## Decisions (mediator, each checked at source)

1. **The exact-zero `resume()` rule is dropped for all categories** in amendment 1, to be
   implemented in the amendment's implementation PR. Probe: an `integrity` halt and an
   `emergency` halt holding the fixture's dust pass the risk check (`ALLOW`) and are
   refused only by the inventory rule, with no automatic way out (`liquidate` refuses dust
   until the bid more than doubles). `exhaustion` and drawdown-past-12% are refused by the
   risk check alone, whatever is held. The rule's residual guard (an emergency halt raised
   at 12–17% against a dust rally) is worth at most one minimum notional, and replay never
   sets the flag. Revision 4's rationale, "exact zero protects halts that stay final", was
   wrong; this record says so. The emergency-at-≥12% asymmetry is kept and disclosed as a
   further owner decision, not an omission.
2. **Halt start, category and reason are captured on the transition only.** The reviewer
   reproduced, and the mediator re-ran: `_halt` is called on every valid frame while the
   risk engine returns `EXIT`, and on every invalid frame during a halt; read literally,
   the spec's "set where the halt is raised" would reset the 24 h clock each frame and turn
   an integrity or emergency halt into a `drawdown` one. Fixed in the spec, with tests.
3. **Engine version and schema:** the amended V0 runs on a successor of `exit-residue-v1`
   and schema 6, assigned by the implementation PR. `exit-residue-v1` names the exit fix
   alone; no V0 result on it was run or inspected (PR #122 ran no replay), so the coherence
   record's "+ V0 on `exit-residue-v1`" trial is the amended V0, annotated in place.
4. **`_resolved()`'s docstring is false at the harvest gate** (it runs before
   `_cancel_buys`), but the behaviour is safe: over 800 constructed states it is never
   true while `exit_state` is `incomplete`; it is the stricter gate. Fix the docstring and
   add call-site comments; **no assert** (Bob's #127 option A would fire on every harvest
   with a resting buy; the issue misread the call order). Follow-up PR.
5. **The census task is not executable for LTCUSDT as written:** its listing hour must come
   from the earliest 1m archive "whether or not it parses", but the only reader it names,
   `read_archive`, raises a plain `DataError` on the unparsed 2017-12, which its own stop
   condition treats as fatal. `read_member` + `csv` is the reader (as the masking and
   outage tasks say). `FeedError` (a transport error, not a `DataError`) has no retry
   guidance. Three `unknown`-rule edge cases were undefined. In-place annotation in the
   follow-up PR; a corrected task file if Bob's current run stops there.
6. **`development_month` accepts unpadded months and `load_spec` then fails open:** `end =
   "2024-6"` is accepted and month iteration runs to 2024-12 (204 files instead of 140).
   Not a window bypass (`2025-1` is refused), a correctness bug. Follow-up PR: full-match
   `\d{4}-\d{2}`, tuple compare.
7. **Bob's #125:** a hashless backtick path is not a pin by design and by test; his fix
   would fail CI on 119 plain mentions in 48 docs. Adopted rule: in a Bob report, every
   `data/*.py` path needs a stated hash or an `UNVERIFIABLE` entry (0 violations today);
   everywhere, an informational count of hashless mentions. **Bob's #126:** the paused
   example is counted today; the real gap is one frame after an exhaustion halt.
8. Confirmed smaller items: `Account.new` → `Account.start`; `integrity` wrongly listed
   "accounting invariant failure" (the store rolls back, never halts); the restart's field
   list omitted `recovery_count` and `draining`; Bob's DSR acknowledgment was conditional
   ("once settled"); the review-fixes record cites pre-#122 line numbers; the coherence
   §2 hurdle table is illustrative and now says so; no handoff record exists for #122
   itself; `flat_stretches.py` mis-measures a series whose first sample is mid-hour (no
   published figure affected); PR #122's "each test fails without its guard" is overstated
   for `funding.py`, whose guard is redundant with `read_member`'s.
