## 1. Walk-forward timing, warm-up and leakage

A fixed rolling **12-month tune / 3-month test / 3-month step** grid is a reasonable development protocol. It does not turn previously examined 2017–2024 data into a pristine holdout. Calling these test windows 'untouched' must be qualified as excluded from that fold's fitting step; human/agent design has already used some of the same history. The existing reserved-window prior-exposure record in spec §7 remains true: reserved replay access is stronger than reused development, but not wholly unseen market-regime evidence.

Before the first evaluated minute of each tune and test period, require at least 200 completed valid daily bars, E's 720 completed reference hours, and **actual readiness of every required indicator**. The current V0 full feature baseline needs 743 completed hourly bars to form 720 complete 24-hour quote-volume samples; a nominal count of 720 alone is insufficient. Apply completeness, market-proxy and breadth availability checks too. Missing warm-up excludes the fold; no padding, future bars or synthetic backfill.

Predeclare tuning candidates, budgets, objective and ties; use only the tune window for that fold's choice. The test result must not change that fold's choice. Later folds may mechanically train on earlier test dates under the predeclared rolling protocol, but all later human-driven changes prompted by those scores are new registered development trials. If making a confirmatory claim about the tuning procedure itself, use a locked outer evaluation; repeated revisions on outer results make that outer evaluation development too.

Freeze boundary handling: positions, pending orders, risk high-water marks, reserves, transaction costs and feature state across folds. Do not erase losses by selecting favorable reset points. Purge any training target or position outcome whose information extends into the test period; never select a purge length after results. Use completed bars and publication delays, including G's actual cadence, gaps and transitions. Score each calendar instant once in the stitched chronological test stream; multiple pairs, intrabar paths and bootstrap replicas are not independent observations.

Keep calendar folds as the primary grid unless a separately reviewed policy says otherwise. Do not move boundaries around the seven known outages merely to avoid difficult periods. Under current integrity rules, invalid spans remain excluded and reported. Choosing to model 'no trading during a verified exchange outage' requires a causal execution/recovery specification first; missing archive bars alone do not establish the cause.

## 2. Synthetic-data integration

Use a **separately labelled synthetic artifact with the same replay/accounting engine and structural integrity checks**. Never present generated files as checksummed Binance archives. Its manifest must identify source archive hashes and time bounds, generator/code/spec versions, seed, donor-block map, regime-label version, transformations, universe/availability mask, derived-output hashes, and explicit synthetic status. Regenerate byte-identically from provenance before trusting results.

Sample one synchronized multivariate source timeline for traded pairs, BTC proxy, breadth, price/volume and funding availability. Define a common regime classification and donor eligibility so 'same phase' cannot select different dates per pair. Preserve gaps and pre-listing absence; do not silently fill absent pairs. Rebuild 1h/1d bars and causal features from generated minutes, rather than pasting precomputed indicators. Enforce finite positive OHLC, ordering, volume/quote-volume consistency, unique timestamps, filter provenance and deterministic tick/lot rounding. Rescaling prices also requires a declared treatment of volume units, notional thresholds and filters.

Funding timestamps must preserve their source settlement interval, publication lag and unavailable states. The proposal's unconditional '8-hour re-stamping' is too broad for the implemented uniform-cadence signal. Explicitly test missing settlements, mixed cadence and boundaries at synthetic block joins; do not convert unavailable G into an available signal.

Freezing regime labels before generation is necessary. Retrospective phase labels are allowed as stress-scenario metadata, not future information available to the strategy. Keeping label order and lengths does not guarantee each resampled phase retains the original realized trend, so report that diagnostic without rejecting losing paths after seeing their returns. Treat this as a regime-conditioned stress generator, not a proof that a globally nonstationary cycle path satisfies stationary-bootstrap inference assumptions. Geometric block lengths are the stationary-bootstrap construction for stationary data; the phase-conditioned adaptation needs its own declared limits. [Politis and Romano, original paper](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~behansen/718/Politis%20Romano.pdf).

Fix an exact primary mean block length before generation; the proposed 3–5-day range is not evidence of an optimal length. Use the fixed block-length sensitivities as robustness checks, not a search for the best result. Scenario failure can reject a supposedly robust candidate under a predeclared rule; synthetic success cannot promote a candidate that fails real-data criteria. Correct the rare-event limitation: the generator cannot invent unseen one-bar shocks outside its empirical support, but repeating/reordering losses can create worse compounded drawdowns than the source path. Scenario loss frequencies are conditional simulation frequencies, not calibrated probabilities of future market losses. Anonymisation reduces recognition; it is no leakage control and must not accidentally change execution economics.

## 3. Multiple testing and the append-only register

Agree to **DSR as the named multiple-testing diagnostic**, rather than selecting among corrections after seeing results. It does not replace C1–C6, cure data leakage or certify future profitability. DSR requires the return-series length, skewness, kurtosis, variance of Sharpe estimates across the tested family and the number of independent trials; a raw count plus one aggregate return per fold is not sufficient. [Bailey and López de Prado, DSR, §DSR and Appendix A.3](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf).

The reviewed spec must fix return frequency, reserve-inclusive equity basis, risk-free benchmark, annualisation, calendar alignment and fold stitching, treatment of no-trade/zero-variance/invalid runs, the tested family, raw and effective trial counts, and the dependence adjustment before any further experiment. Retain aligned return series for every eligible candidate. The existing hourly-equity output can be an input, but its sampling and gap/halt semantics need validation. Serial dependence changes Sharpe uncertainty; naive annualisation and an iid DSR probability must not be sold as calibrated confidence. [Lo, The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios](https://alo.mit.edu/research-page/the-statistics-of-sharpe-ratios/).

Choose any significance threshold and its relationship to selection in the reviewed spec before running; do not quietly add or relax owner acceptance criteria here. Report assumptions, raw trial count and sensitivity to correlated candidates. If an assumption or effective count cannot be supported, report DSR as conditional/indeterminate rather than declaring a pass. Count adaptively inspected strategy/parameter changes, not every seed or path as a new independent strategy. Preserve prior experiments as retrospective entries, explicitly labelled as not preregistered; an incomplete historical search cannot be reset to zero.

Yes, keep `docs/trials/register.jsonl` in the repo, **append-only, established and committed before the first further experiment starts**. 'Before the report is written' is too late. Use separate append events: registration (trial ID, parent/family, time/agent, hypothesis, code/config/spec hashes, parameter grid, data hashes/folds/mask, fees/paths, seeds, budget/stopping and selection rules), then result/failure/cancellation and any correction referencing the same ID. Never rewrite a loser or leave a failed attempt unrecorded. Block dispatch without a valid committed registration, and block accepted reports without matching result provenance.

Implement registration and verified result appends through a separately reviewed trusted process.
