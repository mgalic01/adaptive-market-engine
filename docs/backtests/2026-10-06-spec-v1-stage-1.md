## What the numbers show

This section is descriptive only. It reports what the 8 runs per variant did, and claims nothing about other periods.

- **Every variant loses money on average.** Mean annualised returns run from −10.8% (A) to −2.0% (F). D, which is not selectable, has a positive mean of 4.7%, but its median annualised return is about −8.7% on both paths, and its drawdowns reach 40%.
- **F, the order-flow entry block, comes closest.** It has the smallest loss of all, it is the only single variant that keeps every run's drawdown under C1's 10%, and it beats the ungated baseline in all 8 runs.
- **The inventory cap reduces drawdowns but not losses.** V0's mean maximum drawdown of 12.7% falls to 8.6% with B and 6.5% with C, while both still lose 7–8% a year.
- **The full stack** has the smallest drawdowns of all: 5.6% mean and 8.5% worst. But it trades too little (C5: 0.69 completed cycles a week) and beats the baseline in only 4 of 8 runs (C6).
- **G and H barely move V0** on these windows. G is within 0.2 points of V0's mean annualised return, and H is within 0.2 points.
- **Every variant fell less than holding the coins** (C3 passes 8 of 8 everywhere), and every run was valid (C4).
