awaits independent full-head review. Named writer: Codex Desktop. This replaces
the uncommitted futures-only draft. No historical run or live trading authorized.

## Objective and evidence

Maximize net compounded return within the drawdown and ruin-risk budget. Productive
risk matters more than looser limits. Favorable 10–20%+ months can occur but are not
guaranteed targets. Feature count and effort do not establish an edge.
Preserve frozen V0–V3 implementations, configurations and all failed evidence.
V1 showed grid-cycle gains overwhelmed by inventory exits; V2 showed missed trend
participation and large lifetime drawdown; V3 recorded short losses and larger drawdowns in the sampled higher-leverage arms;
this is not an isolated causal estimate of leverage.
Missed participation is not proof a rejected trade would win. See the published
V1/V2 verdicts, V3 return diagnosis and pending cross-version diagnosis PR265.

## Acceptance

A pass requires higher compounded net return AND better return/maximum-drawdown
than EVERY declared matched earlier baseline for the FULL combined arm;
component/ablation results are explanatory and cannot replace a failed full arm; lifetime observed maxDD <=30%; net profit
positive with doubled execution costs; no liquidation or unexplained accounting
residual; every mandatory comparison present. Zero-DD return/DD is undefined,
reported separately and makes the return/DD acceptance comparison indeterminate
unless a separately reviewed criterion is frozen before execution. Same dates, starting capital, source availability and costs
for comparisons. Never compare incompatible windows or pool repeated paths.
Report 10,000 USDT and approximately EUR100 using a separately pinned EUR/USDT
conversion assumption; 100 USDT must not be labelled EUR100. Exchange minimums
apply in both scenarios. Historical use of present-day saved filters is disclosed.
Report net equity, CAGR, drawdown, return/DD, Sharpe with sampling definition,
concentration and unfavorable periods. Synthetic tests establish behavior, not edge.

## Package B: causal market assessment, schema combined-v1

Each entry assessment records symbol, decision time, source availability timestamps,
daily context, four-hour trend, structure, volatility, extension, transaction costs,
funding, executable size, existing holdings/reservations and correlated exposure.
Only completed information is visible even when future records are loaded in memory.
A missing due bar, warmup failure, stale quote or non-finite input is unavailable.
No favorable assumption and no unmeasured calibrated probability.

Initial engineering preset (fixed before results, not claimed optimal):
- daily and 4h trend: existing SMA20/SMA50, ADX14 threshold20, directional DI;
- breakout: completed 4h close beyond prior20 completed 4h highs/lows, excluding
  current bar; continuation: aligned daily/4h and close on supported SMA20 side;
- pullback: same trend alignment and distance to 4h SMA20 <=1 ATR14;
- volatility: 4h ATR14 / median of previous120 valid 4h ATR14; >=3 means stress;
- extension: abs(close-SMA20)/ATR14, >3 rejects trend entry, >2 halves stop risk;
- quote age <=1 hour and due daily/4h/hourly inputs required for increases;
- futures funding estimate must be already published and <=8 hours old;
- RSI14 recorded, not universal veto. Continuation ablation restores long RSI>=75
  and short RSI<=25 veto, leaving all other conditions unchanged.
Record distance to band in ATR units, data gaps and independent refusal reasons.

## Package C: routing and components

| Assessment | Eligible behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Daily/4h UP | Spot trend; futures long fallback if spot unexecutable |
| Daily/4h DOWN, affirmative bearish structure | Futures short, grid off |
| Daily/4h RANGE, not stress | Spot grid |
| Conflict/transition | Manage old positions, no directional increase |
| Stress/unavailable | Cancel increases and prioritize protective reductions |

One strategy owns an asset across venues and pending orders. No hidden hedge or
spot/futures duplication. Transfer requires close, flat confirmation, release and
explicit new allocation. Spot preference avoids funding; futures-only control
measures futures longs independently. Unknown spot executability is not permission
for futures fallback. Deterministic symbol ordering is recorded, no new optimizer.
Old quarterly selection remains a baseline only.

Trend stop: 2 completed 4h ATR14. Prefill execution-price bounds resize/reject
orders that would exceed approved cash risk. An unexpectedly worse already-filled
price is booked honestly, immediately triggers excess-risk reduction at available
prices, and records the temporary breach and extra costs; it is never retroactively
rejected or erased. Trail activates at2 initial R and follows3 completed ATR14 from
best completed price, never widening. Reversal exits at next executable quote.
Short needs DOWN on both timeframes plus bearish breakout/continuation/pullback.
No countertrend UP short, including ablation. Short ablation permits daily UNCLEAR
with bearish4h, never daily UP. For this ablation only, the DOWN routing row
and bearish structure qualification accept daily UNCLEAR with 4h DOWN; the
other conflict cases still refuse. Structure band/SMA conditions remain unchanged. No short merely because long is absent.

Grid spacing=max(0.5 ATR14, 2 complete estimated round-trip costs in price).
Three equal-risk buy levels below decision price; each lot exits one spacing above
its actual fill. Inventory stop2 ATR below lowest level; total risk shares the
asset cap. No martingale. Rebuild only flat or explicit cancel/reallocation. Leaving
range closes inventory. Include inventory losses with cycle gains. F flow gate uses
15 completed1m bars: block below0.40/unavailable; unblock>=0.45; initial blocked.
Missing flow makes grid buys unavailable, not ungated. Sales bypass flow gates.

## Package D: shared capital, exposure and protection

Equity=free cash+spot marked inventory+futures collateral+futures unrealized P&L.
Collateral transfers consume free cash, not create a second account balance.
Fees/funding debit actual wallet; cash, quantities and transfers reconcile exactly.
Trade-to-equity residual <=1e-18 USDT only, all residuals retained and reported.
Initial risk per asset0.5% equity, aggregate open+reserved stop risk<=3%; asset
notional<=20% equity, portfolio gross<=1.6x, absolute cap2x. Spot cash nonnegative.
Futures collateral>=50% notional. Reserve entry/exit costs. Pending reservations
are atomic across strategies for cash, margin, notional and risk.
Connected groups of abs Pearson daily-return correlation>=0.80 over60 common
completed daily returns have gross cap80% equity. Unknown correlations group all
affected assets conservatively. Same causal snapshot per admission batch.
Adverse estimated next24h funding (last-known rate x3 settlements)<=25% stop risk.
This initial preset admits futures only with a source-verified eight-hour funding
cadence known at decision time; unknown or changed cadence refuses increases.
Actual published funding events are charged regardless of entry eligibility.
Its ablation removes only admission filter, not actual charges/margin controls.
Maintenance uses pinned existing V3 model, not a claim of exact exchange liquidation.
Require margin/maintenance>=4 at admission, reduce below3, liquidation at<=1 fails.

At lifetimeDD>=30% cancel increases and close at available prices. This trigger is
not a guaranteed30% ceiling. Missing bars preserve close obligations, never fills.
Cooldown starts after tradable liquidation completes, not before. This means
no pending reductions and no position that can legally be reduced at saved venue
filters. Dust-only remainder permits the timer but stays valued and owned; stale
prices or unacknowledged orders cannot be classified as dust. Explicitly classified
unsellable dust stays valued and prevents reassignment of its asset.
After>=24h reassess: healthy data, qualified strategy and direction, acceptable
cost/margin required. Time alone never trades. Restart25% normal allowance:
compute normal admissible size then multiply by0.25 and round down; also scale
aggregate risk/notional budgets by0.25 so multiple new orders cannot evade it.
Half-risk uses0.5 in the same manner. Never multiply the same constraint twice;
compare scaled candidate with remaining scaled aggregate allowance.
Recovery episode DD>=3% from its own peak closes and starts another24h cooldown.
Lifetime peak never resets. Half risk only lifetimeDD<15%, normal only<10%, with
qualification. Episode ends on normal restoration. While recovering, its3% stop
governs instead of retriggering the original30% event every tick. All episodes,
refusals and incremental P&L retained; maxDD>30% always fails acceptance.
Accounting/execution integrity failures and capital exhaustion never auto-recover.

Protective management runs on every available price/fill/funding/margin event,
independently of entry gates. Stops never widen. Deduplicate events. Known funding
is charged to pre-timestamp holdings before exits; reductions precede increases;
check again after fills. OHLC: old adverse stop before favorable target; gap at
worse open/stop plus adverse slippage. Do not tighten from same-bar high before
checking its low. Newly filled positions receive adverse same-bar treatment.
Partial fills preserve remaining reservations/close obligation. No fictional fill.
