## Why V4 exists: what the V3.1 run showed

V3.1's one registered run (run 38703911852, verdict FAIL, verdict.json SHA-256
`38e76612b712a7e064c3b21ef081d618701d17017f58e543db3d9d862510881d`) and the read-only
analysis of its 324 results (kept on the owner's PC) showed, for 2021-07 to 2024-12:

- every V3.1 component lost money. The combined system made -19% (10,000 USDT) and
  -25% (1,000 USDT), with worst drops up to 38%. It lost 6% even in the 2020-21 bull
  market (a window that was reported only);
- spot trend lost the most. Only 32% of trades won. Most trades ended at the initial
  stop, two four-hour ATRs away. The profit given back from trade peaks was about five
  times the final loss;
- "pullback" entries were the worst entry type, long and short. Short "continuation"
  entries in a confirmed downtrend were the only clearly profitable entries, mostly in
  2022;
- the grid lost in every year: target exits were wiped out by range-exit and stop
  losses, as in V1;
- the 30% lifetime shutdown fired in 9 of 12 combined cells in March-April 2023. The
  quarter-size recovery that followed lost more;
- switching the trailing stop off improved the result by about 9.5 points, from about
  -24.5% to about -15% at 10,000 USDT and from -25.0% to -15.9% at 1,000 USDT. It still
  lost money. This is corrected 2026-10-19 from complete cells, with the diagnostic replay
  of the crashed ones. An earlier "-19% to -4%" counted 3 cells that had crashed early.
  Removing automatic recovery helped by about 6 points; the other ablations helped less
  or not at all;
- on the same coins and dates, V3's daily long-only rules made +78% to +178%, with
  44-56% drops. Holding made +120% (an 81% drop). V3 as built (the walk-forward selector,
  long and short) made +25% to +28%, with 46-67% drops;
- 8 V3.1 cells at 10,000 USDT stopped early with
  `replay_failure:execution_failure:ValueError`. The root cause was found and fixed in
  PR #287 (2026-10-19): the routine cap check inside a settlement batch could cancel the
  very order being filled;
- the frozen V3.1 aggregate could not run at real scale. It needed 107 GB of disk, about
  75 GB of memory, and one to two weeks to rebuild every report.

V4 keeps what worked in V3 (slow daily signals, volatility sizing, holding until the
signal turns, no shutdown) and in V3.1 (one shared spot and futures account, the market
reading, detailed decision records, exact accounting, margin safety). It drops what the
evidence showed hurt.

## Universe: coins chosen by a rule

- **Candidates.** Every Binance spot symbol quoted in USDT that ever traded, delisted
  ones included. Excluded:
  - stablecoins and fiat tokens: a fixed list (USDC, BUSD, TUSD, USDP, PAX, DAI, FDUSD,
    USDD, UST, EUR, GBP, AUD, TRY, BRL), plus any symbol whose daily close stayed within
    2% of 1.0 USDT on 90% of its last 90 days. This test is evaluated afresh at each
    selection date, over the 90 calendar days before that selection's 00:00 UTC. A day
    without a bar is not within the band, so at least 81 days must be. A pegged coin is
    therefore never judged on prices after its selection date;
  - leveraged tokens: base assets ending in UP, DOWN, BULL or BEAR, where the base without
    the suffix is itself the base of another listed USDT symbol (BTCUP, ETHBEAR), and a base
    that is exactly one of those words (BULL and BEAR, the BTC members of that family).
    Real coins like JUP are not caught;
  - wrapped or staked duplicates: WBTC, WBETH, BETH, STETH;
  - any symbol with fewer than 200 daily bars before the selection date;
  - any symbol with no daily bar in the 7 UTC days before the selection: a dead market,
    as in "Safety". So every ranked candidate has at least one volume observation.
- **Selection.** At 00:00 UTC on the first day of each month, rank the candidates by mean
  daily quote volume (USDT) over the previous 30 UTC days, using only days with a daily
  bar. Ties go alphabetically.
  - The universe takes candidates in rank order. A candidate is skipped if its hourly spot
    data has no unmasked bar in the 168 hours before the selection: it could neither
    update its signals nor fill, so it is a dead market for trading purposes.
  - The first 10 that are not skipped form the universe, considered down to rank 20 of
    the ranking. So the data step fetches hourly spot for every symbol that ranks in the
    top 20 in any month.
  - While fewer than 10 candidates qualify, as in early 2018, or fewer than 10 of the top
    20 pass, the universe is all that qualify: a short universe is permitted.
- **Shorts, and the futures part of longs,** need an eligible USDT-margined perpetual: one
  listed at the decision time (inside its trading span) with a complete funding month
  (see "Data"). Without one, the coin is spot-only and long-only that month. A missing or
  masked perpetual bar at one decision or fill does not change this; the deferral rule
  alone handles it.
- **Leaving the top 10.** The month's selection is made at 00:00 UTC, before that same
  00:00 daily decision. That decision already uses the new universe, so a coin that left
  gets a target of 0 then, and its position fills out at the next hour's open.
- **The selection is recorded every month:** the ranked list, the volumes, and who
  entered and who left.

## Data

From Binance's public archive (`data.binance.vision`), exactly as earlier versions
fetched it, with the existing retry rules:

- daily spot klines for every candidate symbol. They are used for the universe only:
  ranking, the 200-bar history count and the stablecoin test. Signals and volatility use
  the daily bars aggregated from hourly spot bars (see "Signals"), which are the only
  source for every trading quantity;
- hourly spot klines for every symbol that ranks in the top 20 at least once, over its whole
  history from its first month. Signals, including the slower neighbor's 250-bar
  lookback, need bars from before the coin first enters;
- hourly USDT-M perpetual klines and funding-rate history for the same symbols where they
  exist, over their whole history;
- present-day exchange filters, applied to history. They are disclosed as in V3 and V3.1:
  - tick and step;
  - the minimum and maximum quantity: the stricter of `LOT_SIZE` and `MARKET_LOT_SIZE`
    (the larger minimum, the smaller positive maximum), as V3.1's venue rules read them;
  - the minimum notional;
  - for each symbol and each venue, recorded and pinned.

  A symbol absent from the present-day snapshot, such as a delisted one, gets this fixed
  fallback, labelled `inferred` and pinned like the rest:
  - tick = 10^-d, where d is the most decimal places in any of the venue's hourly prices
    for that symbol, after trailing zeros are dropped;
  - step = 10^-d in the same way from its hourly base volumes, and minimum quantity =
    step;
  - maximum quantity = 9,000,000,000;
  - minimum notional = 5 USDT on either venue.

**Hourly bars are validated and masked** with V3's rules (V3 section 2), re-implemented in
V4 because the frozen readers refuse 2025 onward:
- the repairing reader of spec v1 section 5 rule 1 applies to both venues:
  - a duplicate or out-of-order row is dropped, and its hour and the last kept row's hour
    are masked;
  - a close off the hour boundary is repaired under the refined rule, and the repaired
    hour is masked, since no minute data checks it;
  - any other defective row is dropped and its hour masked;
  - an archive with a row whose open cannot be read is unreadable, and its whole month is
    masked;
- every missing hour between a symbol's first and last bar on that venue is masked. Hours
  before the first bar or after the last are not listed at all;
- a masked hour has no bar. It is not traded, positions are carried at the last mark (see
  "Safety"), and it counts towards the dead-market rule;
- a daily bar needs at least 20 unmasked hours of its UTC day (V3's per-day rule).
  Otherwise the day has no bar;
- daily spot archives, used only for the universe, go through the same reader. A masked
  or dropped day has no bar. So a duplicate or out-of-order daily row removes both its
  own day and the last kept row's day, as the reader's rule masks both.

V3's 17% rule, which excludes a whole coin-month, is not adopted for klines. With delisted
coins in the universe, it would drop a delisting month in advance and so foresee the
delisting. Masked hours only remove bars.

**Funding months are validated** with V3's exact schedule rule (V3 section 2):
- every record of the month declares the same interval I (`funding_interval_hours`), and I
  divides 24. A file without that column is read as I = 8;
- the expected slots are 00:00 UTC + k x I hours inside the month that fall within the
  perpetual's trading span: at or after the open of its first hourly bar, and at or before
  the close of its last, over its whole history. The span comes from the klines, never
  from the funding file being judged, so a file that loses its first or last records
  still fails. A listing or delisting month is therefore judged only while the perpetual
  traded, for the same reason as above;
- each record lies within 60 seconds after one slot, and every slot has exactly one record.

A missing file (inside that span), a missing slot, a duplicate, an off-slot record or a
change of interval makes the month incomplete. An incomplete month leaves the coin with no
eligible perpetual for the whole month, as V3's excluded coin-months work:
- no futures exposure is held in it. A futures position closes at the daily decision made
  at 00:00 UTC on the previous month's last day, after the close of the day before. That
  decision fills at 01:00 UTC that day, so the coin is flat in futures through every
  funding timestamp of the month. The close does not wait for a spot daily bar: it
  overrides the no-bar rule, as in V3;
- if that hour's perpetual bar is masked, the close moves to the next unmasked perpetual
  hour before the month starts, as in V3;
- if there is none, the leg closes at 00:00 UTC on the month's first day, before any of
  its funding. The close is synthetic, at the perpetual's last close with the 10%
  dead-market penalty and the venue's normal fee, and ignores the filters. V3 would have invalidated the run; V4
  books the penalised close instead;
- a long's target moves to spot under the spot-first rule, and a short's target is 0;
- futures may be used again from the first decision whose fill falls after the month.

Judging a month from its whole file is a look-ahead, disclosed below, as in V3. Funding is
charged exactly at the records' timestamps.

The window starts at 00:00 UTC on the first month whose selection has at least one
eligible candidate. Daily bars from 2017-08-17, when Binance spot USDT trading began, feed
the history, and every arm starts at that same boundary. The window ends at the last
complete calendar month before the prepare step runs; the exact end month is pinned in the
registration. Data preparation is pinned by SHA-256 like V3.1's packs. Fetch-code tests
must block the network.

## Signals: three speeds from daily closes

Daily bars are UTC days aggregated from hourly spot bars, as in V3 (section 2). Each
coin's signal is computed after each daily close, from completed bars only.

- **Fast:** up if close > SMA(20), down if close < SMA(20).
- **Medium:** the mean of two votes: EMA(21) vs EMA(55) (+1 above, -1 below), and V3's
  Donchian 55/20 state (+1, 0 or -1, V3 rule R3). Up if the mean is > 0, down if it is
  < 0.
- **Slow:** up if close > SMA(200), down if close < SMA(200).

A speed still warming up counts as neither up nor down. Every lookback counts available
daily bars, as V3 section 4 does ("n closes means the last n available bars"). A UTC day
without a daily bar adds no observation and resets nothing.

**A day with no daily bar for a coin** follows V3 section 4's no-bar rule. At the decision
after that day:
- the coin keeps its previous signal and takes no order, so its legs stay as they are;
- any deferred order it already has stays pending;
- the other coins are decided as usual, with its sigma and covariance entries using the
  returns it has.

Two events override this, because they do not depend on the signal: a universe exit and
the funding-month close (see "Data"). Each is made even on a no-bar day, and each cancels
any deferred order the coin still has.

**Market reading, "confirmed downtrend":** all of
- close < SMA(50);
- SMA(50) is below its value 10 bars earlier;
- the high of the last 20 bars is below the high of the 20 bars before them (lower highs);
- the low of the last 20 bars is below the low of the 20 bars before them (lower lows).

**Short fraction S:**
- S = 0 unless the medium speed is down and the downtrend is confirmed;
- otherwise S = (0.5 if fast is down, else 0.25) x (2 if slow is down, else 1).

So medium and fast down gives 0.5; with slow also down, 1.0. A fast rally halves it.
The medium speed turning up closes it.

**Long fraction L:** the number of speeds that are up, divided by 3 (0, 1/3, 2/3 or 1).

**Net signal:** s = -S if S > 0, otherwise s = L. The long-only version uses s = L
always.

These lookbacks are standard settings fixed before any V4 result. They are not claimed
optimal.

## Sizing: volatility targeting, V3's method

At each daily decision, after day d's close, V3's sizing steps 1-3 (V3 spec section 5)
with V4's settings and one change in step 2:

1. raw weight = s x (1 / sigma), where sigma is the coin's annualised volatility over its
   last 60 daily returns. A coin with fewer than 60 returns, or with sigma = 0, gets 0,
   as in V3.
2. The scale comes from the full-strength book, so that agreement sets the size. Let
   `full` be each coin's `sign(s) x (1 / sigma)`, which is 0 where s = 0 or sigma is
   unavailable. Then:
   - `c = 0.40 / sqrt(full' Sigma full)`, with Sigma the 60-day covariance over common
     days, using the **volatility target: 40% a year** (main);
   - every weight is `c x raw weight`.

   So a book whose coins all have |s| = 1 runs at the 40% target, as V3's ±1 signals
   did. Each coin's weight is |s| times its full-strength weight, so L = 1/3 holds a
   third of the full position, and a fast rally halves a short. This is the difference from V3's step
   2, which scales the raw weights themselves and would undo the agreement sizing. If
   `full` is all 0, if `full' Sigma full` is 0, or if there are fewer than 40 common days,
   every target is 0. `full` is 0 for a coin whose raw weight is 0 under step 1.
3. Caps, in order: each coin |weight| <= 0.30; sum of |weights| <= 2.7 (90% of the 3x
   ceiling), scaled down proportionally if exceeded.

**Fewer trades:** a coin trades to its target only if the sign differs from the held
position, or the target is 0 with a position open, or
`|target - current| > max(0.25 x |target|, 0.01)`. Otherwise its position stays.

This test runs at the fill hour, exactly as V3 spec section 5 step 4 and section 6 run
theirs:
- `current` = the coin's signed notional over both venues (spot quantity x the spot
  open, plus futures quantity x the futures open, each at the fill hour's unslipped
  open), divided by equity at that open before the hour's fills;
- the target quantities use the same opens and equity (V3 section 5 step 5);
- if any venue the coin's order needs has a masked or missing fill-hour bar, the coin's
  whole order is deferred under V3's masked-fill-hour rules, including colliding
  decisions and the 00:00 rule;
- the exception is a mandatory exit, which does not depend on the signal: a universe
  exit or a funding-month exit. It closes each leg on that leg's own venue as soon as
  that venue has a bar, independently of the other venue. A replacement spot buy that
  goes with a funding-month exit is a separate order and defers on its own.

## Account and execution: one shared account

- **One wallet** holds USDT cash, spot coins and futures collateral. Equity is cash plus
  spot coins at mark, plus futures collateral, plus futures unrealized profit or loss.
  Money is counted once, as in V3.1 (Package D).
- **Longs on spot first, coin by coin.** All amounts are in USDT, from the targets at the
  fill hour.
  - **Deferrals come first,** before any allocation, and the allocation is computed once,
    never again after it. A coin's order is deferred when a venue it needs lacks the
    fill-hour bar:
    - it needs spot if it holds spot or its target is long;
    - it needs the perpetual if it holds futures, its target is short, or it is long with
      an eligible perpetual.

    A deferred coin, a no-bar coin and a coin the band test leaves alone all keep their
    legs as held. A leg that a mandatory exit closes this hour counts as 0, since it
    fills in the same batch's reductions. The coin's other, deferred leg stays as held.
  - **Fixed holdings.** The spot notional of the coins that keep their legs, dust
    excluded, sums into S_K, and their futures notional, long or short, into F_K. Their
    dust sums into D. None of these is re-split.
  - **Definitions,** over the coins that trade this hour, which are all the others. E is
    the set of long coins with an eligible perpetual and N the spot-only long coins.
    Their target long notionals sum to G_E and G_N, and G_L = G_E + G_N. Shorts sum to
    G_S and are all on futures. Futures need initial margin of notional / 3, and 2% of
    equity is held back for costs. The room left is
    `R = 0.98 x equity - S_K - D - F_K / 3`.
  - **A trading coin's dust** is part of its own spot leg, not of D:
    - its spot order is its spot target minus its current spot, dust included. A sale
      that would fall below the filters is not made, and the dust stays;
    - where it has an eligible perpetual, its futures target makes its signed position
      over both venues, dust included, equal its target. A short beside dust therefore
      shorts that much more;
    - a spot-only coin can miss its target by at most its dust.
  - **Spot capacity:** `s_max = max(0, min(G_L, 1.5 x (R - (G_L + G_S) / 3)))`.
  - **If `s_max >= G_N`:**
    - every N coin is held fully on spot;
    - the remaining spot capacity `s_max - G_N` goes to the E coins pro rata to their
      targets;
    - each E coin's rest is on futures.
  - **If `s_max < G_N`:**
    - every E coin is held fully on futures;
    - every N coin's target is scaled by one common factor
      `k = max(0, R - (G_E + G_S) / 3) / G_N`, capped at 1, and held on spot;
    - nothing is redistributed to other coins.
  - **Cash clip,** as V3's hold benchmark does for spot. The fills go reductions first,
    then increases, each alphabetical by symbol; within one coin, the spot leg goes before
    the futures leg. An increase that the free cash cannot pay for at
    its slipped price is cut to what the free cash pays for, rounded down to the step. A
    spot buy needs its notional plus its fee; a futures increase needs its initial margin
    plus its fee. Free cash is the smaller of:
    - wallet USDT minus the initial margin of the futures positions then open;
    - backing minus 4 x maintenance (see "Safety").

    So no increase, spot or futures, takes the margin ratio below 4, and none is made
    while it is below 4. An increase cut below the filters is skipped and recorded.
- **Shorts on futures.** Historical funding is charged to and paid out of the wallet.
  Longs on futures pay it, and shorts are paid it when the rate is positive.
- **Timing:** decisions after the daily close (00:00 UTC). Orders fill at the next hour's
  open with slippage, as in V3.
- **Costs (normal):** spot fee 0.10%, futures fee 0.05%, slippage 0.05% per fill. These are
  the V3.1 cost profile. Doubled costs double all three.
- **Exchange filters,** following V3 spec section 5 step 5:
  - quantities round toward zero to the step;
  - an order that opens or increases a position is not made if it is below the minimum
    quantity or the minimum notional, and the refusal is recorded;
  - an order above the maximum quantity is split into the fewest orders, each paying its
    fee.
- **Reductions are never refused for size:**
  - a futures reduction (a signal close, universe exit, margin cut, ceiling cut,
    funding-month exit or delisting) is reduce-only, so it ignores the minimum notional. A reduction below the
    minimum quantity is raised to it, or to the whole position. One that would leave less
    than the minimum quantity closes the whole position (V3 section 5 step 5);
  - a spot remainder that cannot be sold because it is below the minimum quantity or the
    minimum notional stays as dust: valued at its mark, reported, and sold as soon as it
    can be (V3's dust rule, as in V3.1).
- **Order of events in an hour:** exactly V3 spec section 6, "Order of events in an
  hour", with one addition placed first:
  - the dead-market closes due at this hour (see "Safety") and any synthetic
    funding-month close due at this instant (see "Data"), booked at the hour's open time
    before anything else. Funding records at or after that instant are not charged on a
    closed leg;
  - the open mark, then the pre-fill liquidation check;
  - the daily fills at the open (reductions first, then increases, each alphabetical);
  - the post-fill mark with the liquidation check, then the margin-cushion check, then the
    leverage-limit check, which is V4's 3x ceiling. Each cut fills at the open and is
    followed by a new mark;
  - funding after the hour's trading, on the positions then held. After each funding
    event come a mark, the liquidation check, the cushion check and the leverage-limit
    check again;
  - the liquidation check at the bar's adverse extremes, all at once.

  As in V3, an excursion inside an hour is not a ceiling breach, and every cut fills at
  the open. V4 places no order inside an hour, so no intrabar price ordering exists that
  could change a result.
- **Window end.** V4's arms and the `hold_*` comparisons close every open position at the
  last hour's close with that arm's normal fees and slippage. The exit reason is "window
  end", and final equity is taken after those fills.
  - A venue without a bar in the last hour closes at its last close, the carried mark.
  - A spot remainder that is still unsellable dust stays valued at that close, with its
    lifecycle censored as "window end (dust)". No synthetic sale is made.
  - `v3_as_built` keeps V3's own ending: positions marked at its last close, censored
    lifecycles and no exit fill (V3 sections 6 and 8). It is the unchanged comparator.

## Safety

- **No tight stop-losses, no trailing stop, no lifetime shutdown, no recovery mode.**
  Positions change only through the signals, the caps and the rules below.
- **The 3x ceiling** is V3's leverage-limit check with m = 3, where gross exposure counts
  spot and futures notional: `(sum |spot notional| + sum |futures notional|) / equity`. It
  runs every hour at the post-fill mark and after funding (V3 section 6). Above 3.0, every
  position is cut pro rata to 2.7 by reduce-only orders at that open, with slippage and
  fees. V4's target of 2.7 replaces V3's 0.80 x m, and spot legs are cut by selling spot.
  - **Positions that cannot trade,** because their hour is masked or they are unsellable
    spot dust, stay at their carried marks. Their gross is the untradable gross. The
    tradable positions are cut by V3's factor
    `k' = (2.7 x equity - untradable gross) / tradable gross`. If k' <= 0, every tradable
    position is closed. A spot remainder a cut leaves below the filters becomes dust.
  - **The limit cannot be restored** if no position can trade (tradable gross is 0), or if
    gross exposure still exceeds 3.0 at the new post-fill mark after a cut. Then the cell
    ends with outcome `limit_unrestorable` (see "Reports"). So does a fill the rules
    require that cannot be made. As in V3 section 8, these are strategy outcomes and fail
    acceptance, not engine errors.
- **Futures margin** follows V3.1's shared-account model (`futures_margin` in
  `combined/execution.py`):
  - backing is free cash, plus futures collateral, plus futures unrealised profit or loss.
    Spot inventory does not back futures;
  - maintenance is 1% of futures notional, as in V3;
  - the margin ratio is backing / maintenance.
- **Margin cushion** (the owner-approved safety rule, now on that backing):
  - **Admission:** the cash clip (see "Account and execution") caps every increase,
    spot or futures, so that the ratio stays at least 4 after its fee.
  - **Cut:** at each point where the 3x ceiling is checked, and just before it, a ratio
    below 3 cuts the tradable futures positions pro rata, by reduce-only orders at that
    open, by `k = (backing / 4 - untradable maintenance) / tradable maintenance`. This
    brings the ratio back to 4 before fees. If k <= 0, every tradable futures position
    is closed. The 3x ceiling check then runs on the new mark.
  - **The cushion cannot be restored** if the ratio is below 3 and no futures position
    can trade, or if it is still below 3 at the new mark after a cut. Then the cell ends
    with outcome `limit_unrestorable`, reason `margin_cushion`, as for the 3x ceiling.
- **Liquidation:** V3's check on that backing, at the open and at the bar's adverse
  extremes, as V3 section 6 does. Futures legs are taken at their adverse extremes at once
  (a long's low, a short's high). The cell is liquidated when backing is at or below
  maintenance. A liquidation ends the cell, is recorded, and fails acceptance.
- **How a cell that stops early ends,** after a liquidation or an unrestorable limit, as
  V3 section 8 ends a run:
  - no later event is simulated, and no closing fill, fee or slippage is charged;
  - every open position, dust included, is marked at the prices of the check that
    stopped it. Masked legs use their carried marks;
  - each lifecycle is censored, with the reason (`liquidation` or `limit_unrestorable`)
    and its unrealised profit or loss;
  - that mark is the cell's final equity.
- **Missing or stale data:** a coin without a fresh bar is not traded. An open position is
  carried at its last mark (V3's masked-hour handling) until data returns or the
  dead-market rule below closes it.
- **Delisting or a dead market:** once a held position's own venue has had no bar for 168
  consecutive hours (7 days, masked hours included), that position closes at the open time
  of the next hour, whether or not that hour has a bar. It closes at its venue's last
  close with a 10% adverse price penalty: below it for longs, above it for shorts. The
  close is the first event of that hour (see "Order of events").
  - The venue is spot for a spot leg and the perpetual for a futures leg, so a perpetual
    that dies while spot still trades is handled on its own.
  - No funding is charged for hours without a funding record.
  - The close is synthetic: it ignores the exchange filters, so a dust remainder closes
    with the rest of the leg, at the same penalised price. The penalty replaces
    slippage, and the venue's normal fee is charged on the penalised notional.
  - **Solvency at the penalised price.** Before any synthetic close, dead-market or
    funding-month, is booked, the liquidation check runs with that leg valued at its
    penalised price. A liquidation there ends the cell. After any close, equity at or
    below zero also ends the cell as liquidated.
  - The rule uses only what was known at the time, never whether data later resumes. The
    penalty is deliberately against the strategy. A coin whose data resumes can enter
    again through the normal rules.
- **Accounting:** wallet and quantity identities exact. Equity-change residual
  <= 1e-18 USDT, kept and reported. A larger residual is an engine failure.

## Versions in the run

| Version | Role |
|---|---|
| `v4_main`: long and short, 40% volatility target | Decides pass or fail |
| `v4_long_only`: s = L | Reported |
| `v4_vol25`, `v4_vol60`: targets of 25% and 60% | Reported: gain vs drop trade-off |
| `v4_faster`, `v4_slower`: signal lookbacks x0.75 / x1.25, rounded (see below) | Reported: neighbor tests |
| `hold_universe`: equal weight in the month's universe, spot, 1x, rebalanced monthly | Comparison |
| `hold_btc`: buy BTC at the start, hold | Comparison |
| `v3_as_built`: V3's walk-forward selector, unchanged, on its own coins and inputs | Comparison |

**The two hold comparisons are spot accounts** with V3's hold-benchmark spot rules (V3
section 8): the spot fee and V4's slippage, the spot filters above, no borrowing, no
funding, and no ceiling or liquidation check. A buy spends its notional plus its fee from
cash, a sell adds its notional minus its fee, and dust follows the dust rule above.
- `hold_universe` rebalances at each month's first daily decision and fills at that day's
  01:00 open, as V4's orders do:
  - a month with an empty universe gives every coin a target of 0: the account sells
    what it can and holds cash and dust;
  - with n >= 1 coins in the month's universe, each gets a target of
    `0.99 x equity / n`, using
    equity at that open before the fills. The other 1% stays in cash for fees and
    slippage;
  - a coin that left the universe gets a target of 0;
  - there is no band: every coin trades to its target each month, subject to the filters;
  - sells go before buys, each group in alphabetical order. A buy larger than the cash then
    available is cut to what the cash pays for, fee included, and rounded down to the step;
  - an order below the minimum quantity or notional is skipped and recorded, and a sell's
    unsellable remainder is dust;
  - masked fill hours, the dead-market rule and the window end apply as for V4.
- `hold_btc` buys BTCUSDT at the window's first fill hour with `0.99 x equity`, under the
  same cash rule, and holds it to the window end.

Each version runs at 2 capitals x 2 cost levels = 4 cells, comparisons included. The total
is 9 x 4 = 36 cells.

The two price paths (high first, low first) of the first draft are dropped. V4 trades only
at hour opens and checks liquidation at all adverse extremes at once (V3 section 6), so
both paths would produce identical results.

**The neighbors scale only these signal lookbacks:**
- fast SMA 20 -> 15/25;
- medium EMA 21 and 55 -> 16/26 and 41/69;
- Donchian 55/20 -> 41/15 and 69/25;
- slow SMA 200 -> 150/250;
- the downtrend's SMA 50 -> 38/62, its 10-day slope -> 8/12, and its 20-day highs and
  lows -> 15/25.

Everything else stays fixed, so the neighbors trade the same universe with the same
sizing:
- the universe's 200-bar history minimum, 30-day volume mean and 90-day stablecoin test;
- the 60-day volatility, the 60-day covariance and its 40-common-day minimum.

`v3_as_built` runs from the first day V3's own pipeline can trade. V3's frozen code
refuses data after 2024-12, so it ends there. Comparisons with it use the common period.
- At 10,000 USDT it runs exactly as built.
- At 1,000 USDT it runs through V3.1's reviewed capital adapter, which changes only the
  starting capital (disclosed, as in V3.1's `unchanged_v3_*` cells). Fills that the
  exchange filters change at the smaller size are part of that disclosure.

## Pass rule (v4-d04)

V4.0 passes only if `v4_main` meets all four, at both capitals:

1. its compounded return / maximum drawdown is greater than `hold_universe`'s (normal
   costs);
2. its compounded return is greater than `v3_as_built`'s over their common period
   (normal costs);
3. its net profit is positive at doubled costs;
4. its equity at the window's end is greater than its equity at 2025-01-01 00:00 UTC
   (normal costs).

**One common measure for every arm.** Compounded return and maximum drawdown in
conditions 1 and 2 use each arm's equity marked at the common hourly boundaries: the open
of every hour at which every price source the compared arms use has its bar, before that
hour's fills. An arm uses a price source at an hour when it holds or trades that
instrument then. This follows V3.1's comparison policy. Hours missing from any source are
skipped for all arms alike and listed. Intrabar extremes drive the liquidation check, but
never this measure.
- **Condition 1** (`v4_main` and `hold_universe`, the same window): the path is the common
  boundaries, then each arm's final equity after its window-end close, as the last point.
  Both arms close at the same hour with costs.
- **Condition 2** (`v4_main` and `v3_as_built`): the path is the common boundaries from
  `v3_as_built`'s first trading hour to the last hour open in its period, 2024-12-31 23:00
  UTC. Neither arm has a synthetic close here: V4 is still trading, and V3's terminal mark
  is not on an hour open.
- **Conditions 3 and 4** use final equity after the window-end close. Condition 4 compares
  it with equity at the open of 2025-01-01 00:00 UTC.
- **Zero drawdown,** as V3 section 8 treats its Calmar ratio. Return / maximum drawdown is
  +infinity when the drawdown is 0 and the return is positive, and 0 when the drawdown is
  0 and the return is 0 or less. +infinity is greater than every finite value and not
  greater than itself.

**Precedence:** a liquidation, an unrestorable limit or an accounting failure in any
`v4_main` cell means fail, whatever else is missing. Otherwise, anything missing means incomplete, never a pass. Each
cell's `summary.json` states its outcome (see "Reports"), so the verdict can tell these
cases apart from the summaries alone.
Maximum drawdown, CAGR, Sharpe and per-era and per-year results are reported only.

**Eras reported separately:** the window's start to 2020-12-31, 2021-01-01 to
2024-12-31, and 2025-01-01 to the end. The report states plainly that only the third era was unseen when
V4 was designed, so a pass means "promising", not "proven".

## Records

- **Daily, per coin in or leaving the universe:**
  - each speed's values and vote, the downtrend reading and its four parts;
  - L, S and s, sigma, and the raw weight;
  - the weight after each step (volatility scaling, coin cap, total cap, band), naming
    the step that changed it, and the spot and futures split;
  - the order placed or the reason none was;
  - fills, fees and funding.
- **Per trade lifecycle:** entry and exit, exit reason (signal, universe exit,
  funding-month exit, margin cut, ceiling cut, delisting, window end, window end (dust)),
  gross P&L, fees, funding, best unrealized profit, profit given back, holding time.
  - A lifecycle is per coin and side. It opens when the coin's signed position over both
    venues leaves zero, and ends when it returns to zero or changes sign. Both tests run
    after a whole batch of fills (the hour's daily fills, or one cut), never between
    the fills of a batch. So a full spot-to-futures move stays one lifecycle.
  - Moving part of a long between spot and futures stays inside one lifecycle. The record
    lists each venue's fills, fees and funding.
  - Best unrealized profit and profit given back are measured on the combined position.
- **Monthly:** the universe selection record and each perpetual's funding-month validation.
- **Hourly:** equity, gross exposure, margin ratio (backing over maintenance).
- **Integrity:** all records hash-chained, in V3.1's `combined-v1` style with a V4 schema
  name. Each cell's bundle carries a receipt.

## Reports

Per cell: an HTML page and a `summary.json` holding exactly the numbers acceptance needs.
For `v3_as_built`, the cell builds it from V3's own records without changing V3's code.

Every cell writes a `summary.json`, even one that stops early. Its `outcome` field is one
of:
- `complete`, in a full summary;
- `liquidated`, with the hour, in a full summary up to that hour;
- `limit_unrestorable`, with the hour and the reason (`ceiling` for the 3x ceiling,
  `margin_cushion`, or `required_fill` for a required fill that cannot be made), in a full
  summary up to that hour;
- `accounting_failure`, with the identity that failed (wallet, quantity or equity
  residual), its hour and the values, in a short failure summary;
- `engine_error`, with the exception's type and message, in a short failure summary.

The verdict treats `liquidated`, `limit_unrestorable` and `accounting_failure` in
`v4_main` as fail. It treats
`engine_error`, a missing summary or a missing field as incomplete. A full summary holds:
- final and initial equity, maximum drawdown, compounded return;
- equity at 2025-01-01 00:00 UTC, and final equity after the window-end close;
- the open mark at every hour of the cell (before that hour's fills), each flagged
  "complete" when every price source the arm uses then has that hour's bar. From these the
  verdict computes the common hourly boundaries of any pair of arms (the hours complete in
  both) and every comparison above, without reading the full reports. At about 70,000
  hours a cell, this is a few megabytes;
- per-era and per-year returns, liquidations, accounting residual;
- the bundle receipt SHA-256.

Run summary: the verdict, the comparison table, per-era results, long vs short, per coin,
neighbors and volatility variants, and a plain-language page.

## Engineering

- **Package:** `src/crypto_grid_bot/v4/`. Reuse by import where the existing code accepts
  V4's inputs: V3's indicator primitives, daily aggregation and covariance sizing math,
  V3.1's shared-account ledger, cost, funding, margin and record schema. Copy into `v4/`
  where it does not. Originals are not changed to suit V4. The one exception is a reviewed
  bug fix with regression tests in non-frozen shared code, as in PR #287. Frozen
  directories are never edited.
- **The V3.1 crash first.** Done 2026-10-19. The root cause of
  `replay_failure:execution_failure:ValueError` is fixed in `combined/engine.py` with
  regression tests (PR #287, follow-ups #288). It is a bug fix in non-frozen shared code
  that V4 reuses, so it is made in place rather than in a copy. The cause is recorded in
  `docs/reviews/2026-10-17-claude-v3-1-verdict.md` (correction) and the PRs.
- **Pipeline:** the V3.1 pattern (plan, prepare, cells matrix, aggregate, status) with
  V4's cells.
- **Each cell verifies its own report** (rebuild and equality, as V3.1's
  `_matching_report` did) inside the cell job, in parallel. Each cell writes
  `summary.json`.
- **The verdict step:**
  - reads only the `summary.json` files and receipts, one cell at a time;
  - keeps a progress file and resumes after an interruption;
  - must not need more than 2 GB of memory or 5 GB of disk;
  - full reports stay in the cell artifacts.
- **Run location and storage:** the registered run executes on GitHub Actions for
  independent, timestamped provenance. Its artifacts are downloaded to the owner's PC and
  checked against GitHub's digests. Deleting them from GitHub afterwards is offered to
  the owner, never done without the owner's confirmation.

## Known limitations

- 2017-2024 was studied before V4 was designed. Only 2025 onwards was not.
- Present-day exchange filters are applied to history. Delisted symbols, which have none,
  use a fixed fallback: tick and step from their own decimals, minimum notional 5 USDT.
- A perpetual's funding month is judged complete or incomplete from its whole file, and
  futures leave the day before it starts: a look-ahead of up to a month and a day. This is
  V3's treatment of excluded coin-months, and it affects only whether futures may be used
  that month.
- The 10% delisting penalty is an assumption.
- Coin selection by Binance volume is a proxy for size. It still omits coins never
  listed on Binance.
- Funding history may start later than futures prices for some symbols. Such a coin is
  long-only until both exist.
- 3x exposure can produce large drops. The owner accepted this (v4-d02, v4-d06).
